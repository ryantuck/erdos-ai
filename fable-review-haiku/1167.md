## Summary

The problem asks a yes/no question in combinatorial set theory: for infinite cardinal λ and finite arity r ≥ 2, does the partition relation 2^λ → (κ_α + 1)^{r+1} imply λ → (κ_α)^r? This is a stepping-up lemma for cardinal partitions due to Erdős, Hajnal, and Rado. The Lean definition of `CardinalPartitionRel` correctly captures the standard semantics (for every coloring of r-element subsets, there exists a monochromatic subset of target cardinality), and the theorem statement faithfully translates the mathematical question. However, two defects were found: (1) the question form is encoded as a bare implication rather than using the `answer(sorry) ↔` wrapper required by this corpus's convention for open yes/no questions (Part A4 defect), and (2) the bibliographic reference [Va99, 7.79] is absent from the docstrings (Part B1 defect, previously flagged in the prior review).
