## Summary

The problem: let 𝔪 be an intermediate cardinal (ℵ₀ < 𝔪 < continuum), and let {f_α} be a family of entire functions (holomorphic on ℂ). If at every point z ∈ ℂ at most 𝔪 distinct values occur among the f_α(z), must the family itself contain at most 𝔪 distinct functions? This is a yes/no question (phrased with "Must…?"), independent of ZFC according to known results. The Lean artifact encodes this as a **bare assertion** (no `answer()` wrapper), which violates the corpus convention for yes/no questions per FABLE_REVIEW.md Part A4. The mathematical content of the theorem statement is sound; only the question-form encoding needs correction.
