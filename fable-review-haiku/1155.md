## Summary

The problem: construct a random graph by starting with Kₙ and repeatedly removing uniformly random triangles until the graph is triangle-free. If f(n) is the final edge count, the original questions (Bollobás–Erdős, [Bo98]) ask: (1) does E[f(n)] ≍ n^{3/2}? and (2) is f(n) ≪ n^{3/2} almost surely? The Lean formalization captures both as direct-assertion theorems (proven by Bohman–Frieze–Lubetzky [BFL15] to be true), each with `by sorry` proof bodies. Semantic fidelity is sound; quantifier structure matches; the encoding of "probability tending to 1" via Tendsto is correct. The critical defect: citation keys are used in docstrings but no bibliography is defined in the module, violating the checklist requirement that every key be defined.
