## Summary

Erdős Problem 1123 asks to prove that two Boolean algebras—P(ℕ) modulo sets of natural density zero (B₁) and P(ℕ) modulo sets of logarithmic density zero (B₂)—are not isomorphic. The problem is ZFC-independent: Just-Krawczyk (1984) proved isomorphism under the Continuum Hypothesis, while Farah proved non-isomorphism under OCA+MA. The Lean file formalizes natural density, logarithmic density, their induced equivalence relations, and quotient Boolean algebras, then asserts non-isomorphism as a bare theorem with `sorry`. The definitions are mathematically sound and well-documented; the critical issue is that the problem's independent status arguably warrants a question-form encoding (`answer(sorry) ↔ ...`) per corpus convention (cf. problem 1005 for precedent), not a bare assertion that presumes non-isomorphism as true.

## A. Semantic fidelity

- Independent back-translation of line 70–71: "There does not exist an order isomorphism between BoolAlgModNatDensity and BoolAlgModLogDensity." This directly encodes the problem's claim that B₁ ≠ B₂ (as ordered structures).
- Types are appropriate: `Set ℕ` is standard for density problems (P(ω) where ω = ℕ); natural and logarithmic densities are defined on ℕ-indexed sequences and finite subsets of ℕ.
- Indexing: both `Finset.range (n + 1)` and `Finset.Icc 1 n` correctly represent their respective ranges ({0,…,n} and {1,…,n}); no off-by-one issues.
- Not vacuous: the statement asserts non-existence of a specific isomorphism; this is a substantive open question (or dependent on set-theoretic axioms).

**A2. Lean semantics traps — pass.**

- Division: all divisions are in ℝ (lines 21, 29). `(n : ℝ) + 1` and `Real.log n` are positive for n ≥ 0; no division-by-zero under `atTop`. Harmless.

**A3. Definitions — pass, one policy note.**

- `HasNaturalDensityZero` (lines 19–22): correctly defines asymptotic density zero as lim |A ∩ {0,…,n}| / (n+1) → 0. Matches docstring.
- `HasLogDensityZero` (lines 26–30): correctly defines logarithmic density zero as lim (Σ_{k ∈ A, 1≤k≤n} 1/k) / log n → 0. The restriction to k ≥ 1 avoids log-division issues; all arithmetic is well-typed.
- `NatDensityEquiv` and `LogDensityEquiv` (lines 34–40): equivalence via symmetric difference is the standard definition of P(ω)/I for ideal I; correct.
- `natDensitySetoid` and `logDensitySetoid` (lines 43–50): **[policy]** These structures contain `iseqv := sorry`. Per the checklist, `sorry` should not appear inside `def`, `abbrev`, or `structure` definitions; only theorem proof bodies. These should be moved to separate lemmas or marked with `by sorry` if that's compatible with the structure syntax. (Note: This is a policy-level suggestion; the mathematical content is sound — density-zero sets do form an ideal, making these equivalence relations valid.)
- No other issues; all definitions used consistently.

**A4. Question form and answer polarity — defect (see A5).**

The problem statement ("Prove that B₁ and B₂ are not isomorphic") is a direct assertion, appropriately encoded as a bare theorem. However, since the problem is **ZFC-independent** (not solved/false in all models), the FABLE_REVIEW checklist precedent (problem 1005) suggests this should be formalized as a **question** using the `answer(sorry) ↔ P` idiom to signal "open/dependent" status. The current bare `¬ Nonempty (...)` assertion presumes the answer is "no they are not isomorphic," which is only true in models satisfying OCA+MA, not in all models (CH gives the opposite answer).

**Recommended fix:** recast as `answer(sorry) ↔ ¬ Nonempty (BoolAlgModNatDensity ≃o BoolAlgModLogDensity)` and rephrase the theorem docstring as a question: "Are B₁ and B₂ isomorphic?" This aligns with corpus convention for independent problems and ensures the formalization is neutral across all set-theoretic models.
