## Summary

The problem asks to estimate F_k(p₁, …, p_u), the minimum count of integers in an interval {n+1, …, n+k} divisible by at least one prime, with particular interest in the range k = αp_u for α > 2. The Lean statement formalizes a known sieve lower bound: for any finite set S of primes and any k ≥ 1, every interval of k consecutive integers contains at least k·(1 - ∏_{p∈S}(1 - 1/p)) - 2^|S| integers divisible by some element of S. The Lean statement and English docstring agree on this formulation; the theorem correctly encodes this sieve-theoretic result. The only defect is a missing bibliography section defining the citation key [Va99].
