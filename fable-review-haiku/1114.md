## Summary

The problem: let f(x) be a polynomial of degree n+1 with n+1 real, distinct roots forming an arithmetic progression a, a+d, a+2d, ..., a+nd (where d > 0). By Rolle's theorem, f'(x) has exactly n distinct real zeros b₀ < b₁ < ⋯ < b_{n-1} in the intervals between consecutive roots of f. The conjecture (proved by Balint) states that the gaps between consecutive zeros of f' are monotonically increasing from the midpoint outward on the right half. The Lean formalization encodes this as an existential over the sequence b with four conjoined properties: strict ordering, all roots of f', exactly these roots, and the gap monotonicity inequality.
