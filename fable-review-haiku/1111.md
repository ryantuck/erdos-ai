## Summary

The problem (El Zahar and Erdős, 1985): Let t, c ≥ 1 be given. Does there exist d ≥ 1 such that for every finite graph G with chromatic number χ(G) ≥ d and clique number ω(G) < t, there exist anticomplete sets A, B ⊆ V(G) with χ(G[A]) ≥ c and χ(G[B]) ≥ c? (Two sets are anticomplete if they are disjoint and share no edges.) The problem is open; known bounds include d(t,2) ≤ C(t,2)+1 and d(3,3) ≤ 8. The Lean formalization correctly captures this as an existential statement over d, universally quantified over all finite graphs, with the custom `Anticomplete` predicate providing the required definition. All quantifier ordering, type choices, and semantic conditions are accurate.
