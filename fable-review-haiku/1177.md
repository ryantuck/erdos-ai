## Summary

The problem concerns 3-uniform hypergraphs and the family F_G(κ) of κ-chromatic
G-free hypergraphs. Three parts: (1) If F_G(ℵ₁) is nonempty, there exists a
G-free ℵ₁-chromatic hypergraph with vertex set of cardinality ≤ 2^(2^ℵ₀);
(2) If F_G(ℵ₁) and F_H(ℵ₁) are both nonempty, their intersection is nonempty;
(3) If κ and λ are uncountable and F_G(κ) is nonempty, F_G(λ) is nonempty. The
Lean formalization encodes all three parts using precise definitions of proper
colorings, chromatic number, and sub-hypergraph-freeness. The definitions are
mathematically correct; statement forms match the source problem structure.
