## Summary

The problem (a problem of Erdős and Hajnal) asks: establish whether four ordinal partition relations hold under the Generalized Continuum Hypothesis or the Continuum Hypothesis. These are partition-calculus questions, asking "does α → (β, γ)² hold?" for various cardinal exponent parameters. The problem is marked OPEN on the source page. The Lean file formalizes all four parts correctly via `OrdinalPartitionPair`, which encodes 2-coloring partition relations using strictly monotone embeddings. However, there is one structural defect: the theorems are formalized as bare assertions of the partition relation under a hypothesis, rather than as yes/no questions using the `answer(sorry) ↔ ...` form required by the corpus convention for open yes/no questions. The fix is minimal: wrap each theorem in `answer(sorry) ↔ (hypothesis →` ... `)` and move the hypothesis into the RHS of the iff.
