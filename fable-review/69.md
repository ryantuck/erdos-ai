## Summary

The problem (erdosproblems.com/69): is $\sum_{n\geq 2} \omega(n)/2^n$ irrational,
where $\omega(n)$ counts the distinct prime divisors of $n$? Status **PROVED**
("solved in the affirmative"): Tao observed the sum equals
$\sum_p 1/(2^p-1)$, a special case of Problem #257, and Tao–Teräväinen [TaTe25]
proved that sum irrational unconditionally; Pratt [Pr24] had earlier proved it
conditionally on a uniform prime $k$-tuples conjecture. The Lean statement
(`conjectures/69.lean:17-20`) asserts
`Irrational (∑' (n : ℕ), if n < 2 then 0 else (n.primeFactors.card : ℝ) / 2 ^ n)`.
Read back independently: the tsum over all of ℕ whose terms vanish for $n < 2$ and
equal $\omega(n)/2^n$ for $n \ge 2$ — exactly the series asked about, asserted in
the true (affirmative) direction, which is the acceptable direct-assertion form
for a solved problem in this pipeline. I found no semantic defects; the findings
are scholarship/polish-level only.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity — pass (source-verified).**

- Recovered verbatim source statement (see Addendum): "Is
  $\sum_{n\geq 2}\frac{\omega(n)}{2^n}$ irrational? (Here $\omega(n)$ counts the
  number of distinct prime divisors of $n$.)"
- Independent back-translation of lines 17–20: "the real number
  $\sum_{n=0}^{\infty} t_n$ is irrational, where $t_n = 0$ for $n \in \{0, 1\}$
  and $t_n = \omega(n)/2^n$ for $n \ge 2$." Since the $n < 2$ terms are zeroed,
  this is exactly $\sum_{n\geq 2} \omega(n)/2^n$. `n.primeFactors.card` is
  precisely $\omega(n)$ (cardinality of the finset of distinct prime factors).
- Indexing: the source sum starts at $n = 2$; the Lean guard `if n < 2` excludes
  $n = 0, 1$ — term-by-term exact, no shift. (Upstream formal-conjectures instead
  reindexes as `∑' n, ω (n + 2) / 2 ^ (n + 2)`; the two encodings denote the same
  series.)
- Types: `Finset.card` is coerced to ℝ *before* division, and `(2 : ℝ) ^ n` is a
  real power with ℕ exponent, so all arithmetic is in ℝ. Correct.
- Not vacuous / not junk: the series is summable — $\omega(n) \le \log_2 n \le n$
  (a product of $\omega(n)$ distinct primes, each $\ge 2$, divides $n$), and
  $\sum n/2^n < \infty$ — so `∑'` denotes the honest limit
  ($= 1/3 + 1/7 + 1/31 + \cdots \approx 0.5169$), not the `tsum` junk value 0.
  The irrationality claim is about the genuine real number.
