## Summary

The problem (Erdős–Hajnal, [Er81b, p.33], $50 prize): let $A, B, C$ be three sets of
cardinality $\aleph_1$; is it true that in any 2-colouring of $A \times B \times C$
there must exist $A_1 \subset A$, $B_1 \subset B$, $C_1 \subset C$, all of cardinality
$\aleph_0$, with $A_1 \times B_1 \times C_1$ monochromatic? The page status is
**DISPROVED** ("solved in the negative"): Prikry and Mills disproved it in 1978
(unpublished), as reported by Todorčević [To94] and Komjáth [Ko25b]. The Lean
(`conjectures/1128.lean:17-23`) is a direct assertion of the true (negative)
direction: there exist types $\alpha, \beta, \gamma$ of cardinality $\aleph_1$ and a
colouring $f : \alpha \times \beta \times \gamma \to \mathrm{Fin}\,2$ such that no
countably infinite $A_1, B_1, C_1$ carry a monochromatic box. Read back independently,
this is exactly the negation of the question's universal statement, so the polarity is
the true direction; I found no semantic defects, only scholarship/polish nits.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity — pass (source-verified).**

- Recovered verbatim source statement (see Addendum): "Let $A,B,C$ be three sets of
  cardinality $\aleph_1$. Is it true that, in any $2$-colouring of $A\times B\times
  C$, there must exist $A_1\subset A$, $B_1\subset B$, $C_1\subset C$, all of
  cardinality $\aleph_0$, such that $A_1\times B_1\times C_1$ is monochromatic?" The
  module docstring reproduces this essentially verbatim.
- Independent back-translation of lines 17–22: "there exist types α, β, γ, each of
  cardinality ℵ₁, and a 2-colouring f of α × β × γ, such that for every A₁ ⊆ α,
  B₁ ⊆ β, C₁ ⊆ γ of cardinality ℵ₀ there is no single colour taken by f on all of
  A₁ × B₁ × C₁."
- Negation bookkeeping, checked by hand: the question asserts
  $\forall A,B,C\ \forall f\ \exists A_1,B_1,C_1\ \exists c\ (\text{mono})$; its
  negation is $\exists A,B,C\ \exists f\ \forall A_1,B_1,C_1\ \neg\exists c\
  (\text{mono})$ — exactly the Lean statement's quantifier prefix and body. The
  ∀/∃/¬ structure is the faithful negation, term by term.
- Existential-over-types vs "sets": the source's universal quantification is over
  arbitrary sets of cardinality ℵ₁; the disproof direction needs only one witness,
  and the property is invariant under bijections (transport the colouring), so a
  single triple of Type-0 witnesses is equivalent to failure for every triple.
  `Type` (= `Type 0`) is inhabited by types of cardinality ℵ₁ (e.g. `(aleph 1).out`
  via `Cardinal.mk_out`), so the existential is not vacuously false on universe
  grounds.
- Types appropriate: `Set α` with `#A₁ = aleph 0` measures `Cardinal.mk ↥A₁`, the
  standard Mathlib reading of "subset of cardinality ℵ₀" (= countably infinite —
  exactly the source's ℵ₀, not "at most countable"). `Fin 2` is the standard
  2-colour codomain. `f (a, b, x)` under the right-associated product
  `α × (β × γ)` is well-formed and hits every triple.
- Properness of $A_1 \subset A$: automatic, since $\aleph_0 < \aleph_1$ forces
  $A_1 \neq A$; no separate hypothesis needed.
- Not vacuous / not trivially true: the cardinality hypotheses on A₁, B₁, C₁ are
  satisfiable (ℵ₁-sized types have countably infinite subsets), so the inner ∀ has
  real content; no degenerate colouring satisfies the conclusion trivially.
