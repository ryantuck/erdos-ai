## Summary

The problem (erdosproblems.com/1043, verbatim from the recovered page): "Let
$f\in \mathbb{C}[x]$ be a monic non-constant polynomial. Must there exist a straight line
$\ell$ such that the projection of $\{ z: \lvert f(z)\rvert\leq 1\}$ onto $\ell$ has
measure at most $2$?" Status banner: **DISPROVED (LEAN)** — "solved in the negative and
the proof verified in Lean." Pommerenke [Po61] (using [Po59]) proved the answer is no,
exhibiting a monic $f$ whose lemniscate projects onto *every* line with measure at least
$2.386$; he also proved every monic non-constant $f$ admits a line with projection
measure at most $3.3$. The pre-fix Lean theorem asserted the **universal positive form**
(`∀ f, Monic → 1 ≤ natDegree → ∃ θ, volume (projection) ≤ 2`) — i.e. exactly the
statement Pommerenke *refuted* — while its own docstring correctly said "Disproved by
Pommerenke". Wrong polarity on a solved problem is a defect (the file asserted a false
proposition as a theorem); fixed by negating the assertion, matching the upstream
`answer(False) ↔ ∀ …` encoding in content. The supporting definitions (lemniscate,
line-projection) are correct.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity — definitions pass (source-verified); theorem polarity defect
(see A4/A5).**

- Independent back-translation of the pre-fix theorem: "for every monic polynomial of
  degree ≥ 1 there is an angle θ such that the Lebesgue measure of the projection of
  $\{z : |f(z)| \le 1\}$ onto the direction $(\cos θ, \sin θ)$ is at most 2." As a
  reading of the *question's universal form* this is faithful; as an *asserted theorem*
  it is the refuted (false) direction — see A4.
- `sublevelSet1043` (line 10) is exactly $\{z : |f(z)| \le 1\}$ (`‖·‖` on ℂ is the
  modulus). `lineProjection1043` (line 15) is the image of $S$ under
  $z \mapsto \langle z, (\cos θ, \sin θ)\rangle$, the standard coordinate of the
  orthogonal projection onto the line through the origin at angle θ. Parametrizing lines
  by angle-through-origin loses nothing: projection images onto parallel lines are
  translates, hence equimeasurable, and $t \mapsto t(\cos θ, \sin θ)$ is an isometry, so
  1-D Lebesgue measure of the coordinate set equals the length measure of the projection
  on the line. θ ranging over all of ℝ (rather than $[0, π)$) is redundant but harmless
  under ∃/∀.
- Hypotheses: `f.Monic → 1 ≤ f.natDegree` correctly encodes "monic non-constant"
  (`Monic` forces `f ≠ 0`, and then `1 ≤ natDegree ↔ degree ≥ 1`, the upstream file's
  formulation — equivalent).
- Not vacuous: the fixed (negated) statement is substantive and true by Pommerenke's
  construction; the positive direction fails, but not trivially (e.g. $f = z^n$ has the
  closed unit disk as lemniscate, every projection of measure exactly 2).

**A2. Lean semantics traps — pass.**

- No ℕ subtraction, no ℕ division, no `Finset.range`, no bounded quantifiers — none of
  the arithmetic traps arise.
- `volume : Measure ℝ` applied to `lineProjection1043 …` is well-defined for arbitrary
  sets (a `Measure` applied to any set yields its outer measure); here the set is in
  fact compact (the lemniscate of a non-constant polynomial is compact since
  $|f(z)| \to \infty$, and the projection map is continuous), hence measurable — no
  measurability subtlety.
- `ENNReal.ofReal 2` (and, in the new variants, `2.386`/`3.3`) are the correct ℝ≥0∞
  comparisons; no truncation issues since the arguments are positive.
- Strict vs non-strict: the source says "measure at most 2" — `≤` is correct; the
  variant bounds "at least 2.386" / "at most 3.3" use `≤` in the right directions.

**A3. Definitions — pass.**

- Both defs match their docstrings and the source notions, including degenerate inputs:
  `sublevelSet1043 0 = ℂ` and constant-polynomial cases are excluded by the theorem's
  hypotheses, not silently mis-measured; `lineProjection1043 ∅ θ = ∅` with volume 0, as
  expected. No `sorry` inside any `def`.
- The defs are used consistently by the theorem and (post-fix) both variants.

**A4. Question form and answer polarity — DEFECT (fixed).**

- The source is a yes/no question ("Must there exist…?") whose answer is **no**. The raw
  corpus style for solved problems is a direct assertion of the *true* direction (per
  the pipeline run file: "Direct-assertion form for a solved problem is acceptable raw
  style if the polarity is the true direction; wrong polarity IS a defect").
- Pre-fix, `erdos_problem_1043` asserted the bare universal positive — a proposition
  Pommerenke proved **false**. The file thus stated a false theorem, and contradicted
  its own docstring ("Disproved by Pommerenke … measure at least 2.386"). **[defect]**
  — fixed to `¬ (∀ f, f.Monic → 1 ≤ f.natDegree → ∃ θ, volume (…) ≤ ENNReal.ofReal 2)`
  (not compile-verified).
- Polarity of the fix checked by hand: the recovered upstream file encodes
  `answer(False) ↔ ∀ (f : ℂ[X]), f.Monic → f.degree ≥ 1 → ∃ u, ‖u‖ = 1 ∧ volume
  ((ℝ ∙ u).orthogonalProjection '' levelSet f) ≤ 2`, i.e. "the universal statement is
  false" — exactly the negation now asserted. Content-equivalent modulo the
  angle-vs-unit-vector parametrization and `natDegree`/`degree` (equivalent for monic).
- No `answer()` in the raw file (correct raw style); no binders before the colon.

**A5. Solution status — pass after fix (source-verified).**

- Recovered page banner: **DISPROVED (LEAN)**, tooltip "This has been solved in the
  negative and the proof verified in Lean." The docstring now records this status, the
  refuting direction, the Lean verification attribution (Alexeev using Aristotle,

  theorem* was inconsistent with this status; post-fix all three (docstring, theorem,
  source) agree.
