## Summary

The problem (erdosproblems.com/1149, from Vardi [Va99, Problem 1.34]): let $\alpha > 0$ be
a real number that is not an integer; then the density of integers $n \ge 1$ with
$\gcd(n, \lfloor n^\alpha \rfloor) = 1$ is $6/\pi^2$. The page status is **PROVED**
("solved in the affirmative"), with the remark "This is true, and was proved by Bergelson
and Richter [BeRi17]." The Lean theorem (`conjectures/1149.lean:35–40`) asserts, for every
real $\alpha > 0$ with $\alpha$ not an integer, that
$\#\{n \in [1,x] : \gcd(n, \lfloor n^\alpha \rfloor) = 1\}/x \to 6/\pi^2$ as
$x \to \infty$ along ℕ. Read back independently, the Lean and the recovered source agree
exactly; the direct-assertion form (no `answer()`) is the right shape for a proved
assertion in the true direction. No semantic defects found — only scholarship/polish nits
(missing bibliography stubs in the module docstring, one redundant qualifier).

## A. Semantic fidelity

  not an integer. The density of integers $n\geq 1$ for which

- Independent back-translation of lines 35–39: "for every real $\alpha > 0$ that equals

  source statement, with "density" correctly unwound as the limit of counting proportions
  (natural density) — the standard reading and the only sensible one here.
- Quantifiers: $\alpha$ is universally quantified as a theorem binder with explicit

  with `0 < α` the negative cases are vacuous but harmless). Binders before the colon are
  fine here — this is a direct assertion, not an `answer()` iff.
- Types: counting in `Finset.Icc 1 x : Finset ℕ`, cast to ℝ before dividing, limit in ℝ.
  Arithmetic happens in the intended types throughout; `(n : ℝ) ^ α` with a real exponent
  elaborates to `Real.rpow`, which agrees with ordinary exponentiation for base
  $n \ge 1 > 0$ — every $n$ in `Icc 1 x` qualifies.
- Indexing: `Finset.Icc 1 x` is $\{1, \dots, x\}$, matching "$n \ge 1$" with denominator
  $x$ — the count is over exactly $x$ integers, so the ratio is the exact counting
  proportion. Term-by-term correct, no off-by-one.
- Not vacuous or trivial: the hypotheses are satisfiable (e.g. $\alpha = 3/2$), the

**A2. Lean semantics traps — pass.**

- ℕ subtraction: none anywhere.
- Division: the only division is `(card : ℝ) / (x : ℝ)`. At $x = 0$ this is $0/0 = 0$

- `Finset.Icc 1 x`, not `range` — correctly $\{1, \dots, x\}$; no off-by-one.
- Floor: `⌊(n : ℝ) ^ α⌋₊` is `Nat.floor`. For $n \ge 1$ and $\alpha > 0$ we have

  `Nat.Coprime` needs.
- Coprimality: `Nat.Coprime n m` unfolds to `Nat.gcd n m = 1`, matching
  $(n, \lfloor n^\alpha \rfloor) = 1$. Edge behavior is faithful: e.g. if
  $\lfloor n^\alpha \rfloor = 1$ then every $n$ counts, as in the source.
- Vacuous bounded quantifiers: none.
- Filter encoding: `Tendsto (fun x : ℕ => …) atTop (nhds (6 / Real.pi ^ 2))` is the
  standard, correct encoding of "the natural density equals $6/\pi^2$" (limit along the
  full sequence of thresholds, so density exists *and* has this value — exactly the
  source's claim).
- No `StrictMono`/`Monotone` or interval-endpoint subtleties arise.
