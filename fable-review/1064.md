## Summary

The problem (Erdős [Er80f]): prove that $\phi(n) > \phi(n-\phi(n))$ for almost all $n$,
but that $\phi(n) < \phi(n-\phi(n))$ for infinitely many $n$, where $\phi$ is Euler's
totient. Status on the recovered page: **PROVED** ("This has been solved in the
affirmative") — Grytczuk–Luca–Wójtowicz [GLW01] proved $A_<$ infinite (and $A_>$ of
lower density $\ge 0.54$), and Luca–Pomerance [LuPo02] proved $A_>$ has density 1. The
Lean file states part (a) as the counting ratio
$\#\{2 \le m \le N+1 : \phi(m) > \phi(m-\phi(m))\}/(N+1) \to 1$ and part (b) as
$\{n \ge 2 : \phi(n) < \phi(n-\phi(n))\}$ infinite. Read back independently, both match
the source's two assertions in the true (proved) direction; the $n \ge 2$ guard perturbs
the sets only at $n \le 1$, which affects neither density nor infinitude. No semantic
defects found; polish applied (docstring enrichment, redundant qualification, four
page-confirmed variants).

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity — pass (source-verified).**

- Recovered verbatim statement: "Prove that $\phi(n)>\phi(n-\phi(n))$ for almost all
  $n$, but that $\phi(n)<\phi(n-\phi(n))$ for infinitely many $n$, where $\phi$ is
  Euler's totient function." The module docstring reproduces it essentially verbatim.
- Independent back-translation of `erdos_problem_1064a`: `countSat1064 P (N+1)` counts
  $m \in \{1,\dots,N+1\}$ with $P(m)$ (`range (N+1)` gives Lean $n \in \{0..N\}$,
  predicate applied at $n+1$ — term-by-term exact for the first index $m = 1$), so the
  statement is: the proportion of $m \le N+1$ with $m \ge 2$ and
  $\phi(m) > \phi(m - \phi(m))$ tends to 1. "Almost all" = natural density 1 is the
  standard Erdős reading, and the counting-quotient limit is this repo's house encoding
  of density (cf. `conjectures/696.lean`, same `countSat` pattern).
- Back-translation of `erdos_problem_1064b`: the set of $n \ge 2$ with
  $\phi(n) < \phi(n-\phi(n))$ is infinite — exactly the second assertion.
- Guard analysis: $n > \phi(n) \iff n \ge 2$ (for $n \ge 2$, $\phi(n) \le n-1$; at
  $n \in \{0,1\}$, $\phi(n) = n$) — the docstring's parenthetical is correct. Unguarded,
  part (a)'s predicate would also hold at $n = 1$ only via Mathlib's junk value
  $\phi(0) = 0$ ($\phi(1) = 1 > 0$), and part (b)'s predicate holds at no $n \le 1$
  ($0 < 0$, $1 < 0$ both false). So the guard changes the counted set at a single point
  (density unaffected) and the infinite set not at all — faithful, and it keeps the
  statement from leaning on `Nat.totient 0 = 0`.
- Not vacuous/trivial: density-1 and infinitude claims are substantive; verified
  the witness family for (b) by hand: $n = 15\cdot 2^k$, $k \ge 1$:
  $\phi(n) = \phi(15)\phi(2^k) = 8 \cdot 2^{k-1} = 4\cdot 2^k$;
  $n - \phi(n) = 11\cdot 2^k$; $\phi(11\cdot 2^k) = 10\cdot 2^{k-1} = 5\cdot 2^k >
  4\cdot 2^k$. (At $k = 0$: $\phi(15) = 8 > \phi(7) = 6$, so $15 \notin A_<$ — the
  page's $k \ge 1$ restriction is necessary and the docstring keeps it.)

**A2. Lean semantics traps — pass.**

- **ℕ subtraction** `n - n.totient`: under the guard $n \ge 2$ we have
  $\phi(n) < n$, so no truncation and $n - \phi(n) \ge 1$; the subtraction is the
  intended one. (Even unguarded it would truncate only at $n = 0$, harmlessly.)
- **Division**: real division by $(N+1) \ne 0$ for all $N$ — the `(N + 1)` indexing
  sidesteps the $N = 0$ division-by-zero junk value entirely (an improvement over a
  plain `/N` encoding).
- **`Finset.range (N+1)`** with predicate at `n + 1`: counts $m \in \{1,\dots,N+1\}$;
  numerator domain and denominator $N+1$ agree — no off-by-one (checked at $N = 0$:
  counts $m = 1$ only, denominator 1).
- **Vacuous quantifiers**: none.
- **Filter encoding**: `Tendsto _ atTop (nhds 1)` is the standard, correct encoding of
  the density limit; `Set.Infinite` is the standard encoding of "infinitely many".
- No `StrictMono`/`Monotone` or interval-endpoint issues arise.
