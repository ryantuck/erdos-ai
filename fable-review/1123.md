## Summary

The problem (Erdős–Ulam, [VMR80, p.238] Question 48, [Er81b, p.30], \$100
prize): let $B_1$ be the Boolean algebra of sets of integers modulo sets of
(natural) density $0$ and $B_2$ the Boolean algebra of sets modulo sets of
logarithmic density $0$; prove that $B_1$ and $B_2$ are not isomorphic. The
page's status banner is **INDEPENDENT** ("Independent of the usual axioms of
set theory (ZFC)"): Just–Krawczyk [JuKr84] proved under CH that the algebras
*are* isomorphic, and (later page edition) Farah [Fa00, Cor. 3.4.4] proved
under OCA + MA that they are *not*, with [ARS85] supplying Con(OCA + MA). The
Lean file (`conjectures/1123.lean`) defines both density-zero predicates
correctly, builds the two quotient types, and asserts
`¬ Nonempty (BoolAlgModNatDensity ≃o BoolAlgModLogDensity)` — the direction
the source asks to prove. The intended reading agrees with the source, but
the statement as written does not *have* that reading: the `≃o` refers to
`LE` structure supplied by `instance : BooleanAlgebra … := sorry` (lines
58–59) — a `sorry` in **data** position — so the order on the quotients, and
with it the truth-conditions of the theorem, is left completely unspecified.
That is a Part A defect, fixed in v2 by replacing the postulated instances
with concrete inclusion-modulo-ideal orders.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity — intended reading pass (source-verified); actual
formal content defective (see A3).**

- Recovered verbatim source statement (Addendum): "Let $B_1$ be the Boolean
  algebra of sets of integers modulo sets of density $0$ (that is, in which
  two sets are equivalent if and only if they differ by a set of density $0$)
  and let $B_2$ be the Boolean algebra of sets modulo sets of logarithmic
  density $0$. Prove that $B_1$ and $B_2$ are not isomorphic."
- Independent back-translation of lines 17–72: natural density zero is
  encoded as $\#(A\cap\{0,\dots,n\})/(n+1)\to 0$ (lines 19–22) and log
  density zero as $(\sum_{k\in A,\,1\le k\le n} 1/k)/\log n \to 0$ (lines
  26–30); two sets are equivalent iff their symmetric difference lies in the
  respective ideal (lines 34–40); $B_1, B_2$ are the quotient types (lines
  53–56); the theorem asserts no order isomorphism exists (lines 70–72).
  Both density encodings are the standard definitions (limit of a nonnegative
  ratio equal to 0 ⟺ upper density 0, so the encoded ideal is exactly the
  density-zero ideal $\mathcal{Z}_0$ and its logarithmic analogue).
- "Sets of integers" vs `Set ℕ`: the standard reading — all references treat
  $\mathcal{P}(\omega)/I$ — so `Set ℕ` is correct; noted in the v2 docstring.
- **The defect:** `symmDiff`-based equivalences and quotients are right, but
  the `≃o` in the theorem elaborates its two `LE` arguments from the
  `BooleanAlgebra` instances declared `:= sorry` (lines 58–59). A `sorry` at
  data position is an unspecified term: *nothing in the file ties the order
  on the quotients to density*. Concretely, the statement's truth value is
  not determined by the mathematics of the problem — an interpretation could
  equip both quotient types (each of cardinality $2^{\aleph_0}$) with, say,
  Boolean structures transported from $\mathcal{P}(\omega)$ along arbitrary
  bijections, making them isomorphic, or with non-isomorphic structures. The
  formal sentence therefore fails to express the Erdős–Ulam question. This is
  the raw-file analogue of the styled file's `axiom instBooleanAlgebra…`
  (same flaw there; see Part E).
- Not vacuous/trivial *under the intended reading*: with the genuine quotient
  orders, non-isomorphism is the substantive independent-of-ZFC statement.

**A2. Lean semantics traps — pass.**

- **ℕ subtraction:** none (all arithmetic on ℝ after casts). ✓
- **Division:** `((n:ℝ)+1)` is never 0. `Real.log n = 0` at `n = 0, 1`, so
  the log-density ratio divides by zero there and yields Lean's junk value 0
  — harmless under `atTop`. `(1:ℝ)/(k:ℝ)` has `k ≥ 1` from `Icc 1 n`. ✓
- **`Finset.range (n+1)`** is `{0,…,n}`, matching the docstring's
  `|A ∩ {0,...,n}|`; `Finset.Icc 1 n` matches `1 ≤ k ≤ n`. No off-by-one. ✓
- **Vacuous quantifiers:** none; degenerate first terms of both sequences
  (values at `n = 0`) are washed out by `atTop`. ✓
- **Filter encodings:** `Tendsto _ atTop (nhds 0)` is the standard, correct
  encoding of both density-zero notions; since the quantities are
  nonnegative, "limit is 0" coincides with "upper density is 0", which is
  what membership in the ideal requires. ✓
- `symmDiff` on `Set ℕ` is the set-theoretic symmetric difference via the
  Boolean-algebra structure on `Set`. ✓
- Decidability of `filter (· ∈ A)` is supplied by `open Classical`. ✓

**A3. Definitions — DEFECT.**

- `HasNaturalDensityZero`, `HasLogDensityZero`, `NatDensityEquiv`,
  `LogDensityEquiv` each match their docstrings and the source, including
  degenerate inputs. ✓
- **`sorry` inside `def`s:** `natDensitySetoid.iseqv := sorry` (line 45) and
  `logDensitySetoid.iseqv := sorry` (line 50) violate A3's rule directly.
  These two are *proof*-position sorries of true propositions (density-zero
  sets form ideals), so they are semantically harmless (by proof
  irrelevance the quotient types are unaffected) — but they belong in
  `sorry`d theorems, not inside `def`s. **[defect]**, fixed in v2.
- **`instance : BooleanAlgebra … := sorry` (lines 58–59): the serious case.**
  These are *data*-position sorries — an entire Boolean-algebra structure,
  including the `≤` the main theorem quantifies over, is left unspecified
  (see A1). **[defect]**, fixed in v2 by concrete
  inclusion-modulo-ideal `LE` instances via `Quotient.lift₂`, with the
  well-definedness facts as `sorry`d theorems.

**A4. Question form and answer polarity — pass (form), with the encoding
note required.**

- The source is a **directive** ("Prove that $B_1$ and $B_2$ are not
  isomorphic"), i.e. direct-assertion form — not a yes/no question — so a
  bare proposition without `answer()` is the right shape for this corpus
  (which has no `answer()` elaborator in any case), and the asserted
  direction (non-isomorphism) is exactly the direction the source asks for.
  Polarity verified by hand: the Lean proposition true ⟺ $B_1 \not\cong B_2$
  (under the intended orders, and granting order-iso ⟺ Boolean-algebra iso,
  which holds since a Boolean algebra's operations are order-determined). ✓
- Because the status is INDEPENDENT, neither this direction nor its negation
  is provable (the CH direction of [JuKr84] and the OCA+MA direction of
  [Fa00] are both consistent with ZFC); the `sorry` records the statement,
  not a truth claim. The raw file's docstring already flags independence; v2
  adds an explicit encoding note. (The styled corpus instead recast it as
  `answer(sorry) ↔ Nonempty (…)` — also defensible *there*; not applicable
  in this raw corpus.)
- No binders before the colon; no `answer()` in the file. ✓

**A5. Solution status — pass (source-verified), enrichment applied.**

- The raw docstring's claims — "independent of ZFC"; "Just and Krawczyk
  [JuKr84] proved under the continuum hypothesis that these two algebras ARE
  isomorphic" — both match the recovered page. ✓
- Incomplete as stated: independence needs the *other* direction too. The

  docstring; added in v2.

## E. Prior-review audit (`deepmind/ai-review/1123.md`)

Written against the styled copy (`deepmind/deepmind/1123.lean`, which uses
`axiom`s and `answer(sorry) ↔ Nonempty (…)`); its mathematics audited claim
by claim:

1. **The central defect was seen and waved through.** §6 states "The axioms
   asserting `BooleanAlgebra` instances are correct ✅" with only the mild
   caveat that "the formalization trusts that the quotient operations are
   well-defined". This misses the point entirely: an axiom (or `sorry`)
   postulating bare *data* `BooleanAlgebra Q` does not postulate "the
   quotient operations" at all — it postulates *some* Boolean structure,
   connected to density by nothing. Under such axioms the isomorphism
   statement no longer expresses the Erdős–Ulam question (its truth value
   can be settled either way by choosing interpretations of the axioms, an
   indeterminacy unrelated to the CH/OCA set-theoretic one the review
   discusses at length in §5). Same failure class as the corpus's recurring
   "wrong claims about … semantics" pattern (cf. the 1002 audit): the review
   analyzes the deep independence subtlety while endorsing an encoding whose
   flaw is one level shallower.
2. **Order-iso claim — right conclusion, sloppy statement.** §6: "an order
   isomorphism is equivalent to a Boolean algebra homomorphism (since the
   lattice operations are determined by the partial order)". The correct
   statement (re-derived): an order *isomorphism* between Boolean algebras
   coincides with a Boolean-algebra *isomorphism* — joins/meets are
   categorical sups/infs, complements and ⊤/⊥ are order-definable. A mere
   homomorphism is not equivalent to an order iso. The conclusion (using
   `≃o` is the right notion) is correct and is relied on by this review's v2
   encoding.
3. **Reuse remark invents a discrepancy (minor).** §1 describes "a minor
   off-by-one in the counting convention" between the file's
   `range (n+1)` / `(n+1)` and the library's count of `{x | x < b}` divided
   by `|{x | x < b}|`, then excuses it as "asymptotically equivalent". At
   `b = n + 1` the two counts are *identical* (`{x | x < n+1} = {0,…,n}`,
   size `n+1`) — on its own description there is no discrepancy to excuse.
   Same invent-then-excuse pattern as the 1000 audit. (Whether the library's
   `HasDensity` matches on other axes is DEFERRED — the library is absent
   here.)
4. **Citations section — accurate and useful.** Its findings (missing
   [Fa00], [VMR80], [Er81b]; \$100 prize; Erdős–Ulam joint attribution) all
   check out against the recovered page/`latex` captures, and all were
   subsequently fixed in the styled file. Its "[JuKr84] … 285 … ✅ Matches
   the website" slightly over-claims: the recovered `/latex/1123` extraction
   has no volume numbers, so "285" is not site-verified (it is consistent
   with reviewer knowledge).
5. **§3/§5 independence discussion — sound.** The description of the
   CH vs OCA+MA situation, the observation that Erdős conjectured
   non-isomorphism while the styled file asks the question form, and the
   "formalization of the question rather than a provable theorem" framing
   are all correct (modulo finding 1: with honest instances).
