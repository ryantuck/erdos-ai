## Summary

The problem: let $K_t(r)$ be the complete $t$-partite $t$-uniform hypergraph with $r$
vertices in each class; is it true that
$\mathrm{ex}_t(n, K_t(r)) \geq n^{t - r^{1-t} - o(1)}$ for all $t, r$? The status is
OPEN. The Lean file defines $t$-uniformity and containment of a $K_t(r)$-copy for edge
sets `E : Finset (Finset (Fin n))`, and asserts: for all $t, r \ge 2$ and $\varepsilon
> 0$, for all sufficiently large $n$ there is a $K_t(r)$-free $t$-uniform hypergraph on
$n$ vertices with at least $n^{t - r^{1-t} - \varepsilon}$ edges. Read back
independently, this is exactly the asked lower bound with the $o(1)$ in the exponent
unfolded in the standard way, stated in the asked ("yes") direction per this corpus's
convention for open yes/no questions (cf. fable-review/1155). The $t, r \ge 2$
restriction against the page's literal "for all $t,r$" is forced (the claim is *false*
at $r = 1$), and the docstring already discloses it. I found no semantic defects; only
polish-level enrichment was applied in v2.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity — pass (source-verified).**

- Recovered verbatim source statement (see Addendum): "Let $K_{t}(r)$ be the complete
  $t$-partite $t$-uniform hypergraph with $r$ vertices in each class. Is it true
  that\[\mathrm{ex}_t(n,K_t(r)) \geq n^{t-r^{1-t}-o(1)}\]for all $t,r$?"
- Independent back-translation of `conjectures/1158.lean:51-58`: for every $t, r \ge 2$
  and $\varepsilon > 0$ there is $N_0$ such that every $n \ge N_0$ carries a
  $t$-uniform, $K_t(r)$-free edge family with
  $|E| \ge n^{t - r^{1-t} - \varepsilon}$. Since
  "$\mathrm{ex}_t(n, K_t(r)) \ge f(n)$" means precisely "some $K_t(r)$-free
  $t$-uniform hypergraph on $n$ vertices has $\ge f(n)$ edges", and
  "$\ge n^{t-r^{1-t}-o(1)}$" unfolds to "for every $\varepsilon>0$, eventually
  $\ge n^{t-r^{1-t}-\varepsilon}$", the Lean matches the English exactly.
- Quantifier order: $N_0$ depends on $t, r, \varepsilon$ — the correct reading (the
  $o(1)$ is as $n \to \infty$ at fixed $t, r$); $E$ depends on $n$ — correct.
- **The $t, r \ge 2$ restriction vs the page's "for all $t,r$"** — verified by hand
  that the restriction is *necessary*, not a silent weakening of substance: for
  $r = 1$, every edge $e$ of a $t$-uniform hypergraph is itself a $K_t(1)$-copy
  (partition $e$ into singletons), so $\mathrm{ex}_t(n, K_t(1)) = 0$ and the literal
  claim $0 \ge n^{t-1-\varepsilon}$ is false; for $t = 1$, $K_1(r)$-freeness means
  fewer than $r$ singleton edges, $\mathrm{ex}_1(n, K_1(r)) = r-1 \ge 1 \ge
  n^{-\varepsilon}$, trivially true. So $t, r \ge 2$ is exactly the nondegenerate
  content of "for all $t, r$". The input docstring states the restriction explicitly
  ("Formally: for all t ≥ 2, r ≥ 2 …"), so there is no drift between docstring and
  code; v2 additionally documents *why* (Addendum fix 2).
- Not vacuous: for $t, r \ge 2$ the statement is the genuine open lower-bound problem;
  the trivial witness $E = \emptyset$ fails the cardinality bound, and taking all
  $t$-subsets fails $K_t(r)$-freeness for $n \ge tr$.

**A2. Lean semantics traps — pass.**

- No ℕ subtraction anywhere: the exponent is computed wholly in ℝ —
  `(t : ℝ) - (r : ℝ) ^ (1 - (t : ℝ)) - ε` (line 58), with `1 - (t : ℝ)` a *real*
  subtraction (negative for $t \ge 2$, as intended), so `(r : ℝ) ^ (1 - (t : ℝ))` is
  `Real.rpow` of a positive base with a negative exponent $= 1/r^{t-1}$. Correct.
- No division; no `Finset.range`; no off-by-one surface at all (no sequence indexing).
- rpow at degenerate base: if $N_0$ were 0 and $n = 0$, `(0 : ℝ) ^ x = 0` for
  $x \ne 0$ (and $1$ at $x = 0$, reachable only at the single value
  $\varepsilon = t - r^{1-t}$); either way the prover may simply choose $N_0 \ge 1$ —
  the existential over $N_0$ makes every boundary case harmless. For $t, r \ge 2$ the
  exponent $t - r^{1-t} - \varepsilon$ is $\ge 3/2 - \varepsilon$, and
  $n^{t - r^{1-t}} \le \binom{n}{t}$-scale is consistent (no impossible demand).
- Vacuous quantifiers: `HasKtrCopy1158` has degenerate behavior at $r = 0$ (always
  true via empty classes) and $t = 0$ (true iff $\emptyset \in E$), both excluded by
  the theorem's $t, r \ge 2$ hypotheses; now documented in the v2 docstring
  (Addendum fix 3).
- "For all sufficiently large $n$" encoded as `∃ N₀, ∀ n, N₀ ≤ n → …` — standard and
  correct.
- Inequalities: `≥` matches the source's $\ge$; `2 ≤ t`/`2 ≤ r` non-strict as
  intended. No `StrictMono`/`Monotone` or interval-endpoint issues arise.

**A3. Definitions — pass.**

- `IsUniformHypergraph1158` (lines 28–29) matches its docstring and the standard

- `HasKtrCopy1158` (lines 34–39) matches its docstring: $t$ pairwise disjoint classes

  transversals of subclasses are transversals of the original classes). This is the
