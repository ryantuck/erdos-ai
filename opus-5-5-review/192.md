## Summary

The page (SOLVED) asks for which $d$ every walk in $\mathbb R^d$ with positive unit steps contains a three-term progression; the
answer is $d\le3$. It explains the equivalence with abelian squares, claims that every ternary string of length $7$ has one, and
cites Keränen's four-letter construction [Ke92].

The Lean defines abelian squares in infinite words by multiset equality of consecutive blocks, and asserts that every word over
`Fin 3` has one and some word over `Fin 4` has none.
