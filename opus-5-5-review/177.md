  $f:\mathbb{N}\to\{-1,1\}$ such that, for every $d\geq 1$,\[\max_{P_d}\left\lvert \sum_{n\in P_d}f(n)\right\rvert\leq h(d),\]where

    Beck's "$h(d)\le d^{8+\varepsilon}$" is an asymptotic statement; v2's `variants.beck` states it with a constant.
