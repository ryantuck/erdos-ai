## Summary

The page (SOLVED) asks for the order of growth of $F(N)$, the largest non-averaging subset of $\{1,\dots,N\}$, and records
$N^{1/4}\ll F(N)\ll N^{1/4+o(1)}$ ([Bo89], [PhZa24]).

The Lean defines non-averaging sets (no $a\in A$ is the mean of a subset of $A\setminus\{a\}$ with at least two elements),
$F(N)$ as an `sSup`, and asserts $N^{1/4-\varepsilon}\le F(N)\le N^{1/4+\varepsilon}$ eventually, for every $\varepsilon>0$.
