## Summary

The page asks whether, for disjoint $A,B\subset\mathbb R^2$ with $|A|=n$, $|B|=n-3$ and $A$
not collinear, some line contains $\ge2$ points of $A$ and none of $B$. The answer is no.
The Lean statement encodes this question exactly, but asserts "yes" for all $n\ge3$, which
is false. v2 asserts the negation. Every other ingredient is correct:

- the line encoding;
- `n - 3` with $n\ge3$;
- disjointness;
- non-collinearity.
