## Summary

The source (OPEN) reads: "Let $f(N)$ be the size of the largest subset
$A\subseteq\{1,\ldots,N\}$ such that every $n\in A+A$ is squarefree. Estimate $f(N)$. In

question, in its asked direction. That is the corpus convention for open yes/no
