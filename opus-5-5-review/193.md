## Summary

The page (OPEN at capture) asks whether every infinite walk in $\mathbb Z^3$ with steps from a finite set contains three collinear
points. Gerver and Ramsey proved yes for $\mathbb Z^2$ and bounded collinear runs in $\mathbb Z^3$ [GeRa79].

The Lean defines collinearity of integer points by linear dependence of differences, and asserts: for every finite $S$ and every
injective $S$-walk, some three walk points $a_i,a_j,a_k$ ($i<j<k$) are collinear.
