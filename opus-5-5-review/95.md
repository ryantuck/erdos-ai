## Summary

The page states (PROVED, \$500) that for every $\varepsilon>0$, $n$ points in the plane
satisfy $\sum_i f(u_i)^2\ll_\varepsilon n^{3+\varepsilon}$. Here $f(u)$ counts the pairs at
distance $u$. Guth and Katz proved $\ll n^3\log n$.

The Lean statement sums the squared *ordered*-pair multiplicities, $(2f(u))^2$, over the
distinct distances, and bounds the result by $C\,|P|^{3+\varepsilon}$. That is equivalent to
the page's statement. v2 keeps it byte-identical and adds references, the current PROVED
(LEAN) status, and two variants.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Back-translation:** "For every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $C>0$ such that for every
  finite $P\subset\mathbb R^2$, $\sum_{d}m(d)^2\le C|P|^{3+\varepsilon}$. Here $d$ ranges
  over the distinct distances of $P$, and $m(d)=\#\{(p,q)\in P^2:p\ne q,\ |pq|=d\}$."
- $m(d)=2f(d)$, so the sum is $4\sum f^2$, and the constant absorbs the 4.
- Distinct points are automatic, because `Finset` has no repeats. That matches the source's
  $x_1,\dots,x_n$ as a set of points.
- **Quantifier order** $\forall\varepsilon>0\ \exists C>0\ \forall P$ is $\ll_\varepsilon$.
  It is not the stronger uniform-in-$\varepsilon$ form.
- **Not vacuous or trivial.** For the regular $n$-gon $\sum f^2\sim n^3/2$ (see #94), so the
  exponent cannot go below 3.

**A2. Lean semantics traps: pass.**

- `(P.card : ℝ) ^ ((3 : ℝ) + ε)` is `Real.rpow` with base $\ge0$. At $|P|=0$ it is

- The ℕ-valued sum is cast to ℝ after summing, which is harmless.
- `P.product P` filtered by `≠` is `P.offDiag`. The filter in `distMultiplicity95` matches
  the image in `distinctDistances95`.
