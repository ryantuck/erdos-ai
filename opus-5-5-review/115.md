## Summary

The page asks (PROVED, Eremenko–Lempert [ErLe94]) whether every degree-$n$ polynomial with
connected lemniscate $E=\{|p|\le1\}$ has $\max_E|p'|\le(\tfrac12+o(1))n^2$. Read literally,
the answer is no; for monic $p$ it is yes. The page's remarks and every downstream
formalization adopt the monic reading.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: DEFECT (fixed).**

- **Source** (verbatim): "If $p(z)$ is a polynomial of degree $n$ such that
  $\{z : \lvert p(z)\rvert\leq 1\}$ is connected then is it true that
  $\max_{z\in\mathbb{C},\ \lvert p(z)\rvert\leq 1} \lvert p'(z)\rvert \leq
  (\tfrac{1}{2}+o(1))n^2$?"
- **Back-translation of the input:** "for every $\varepsilon>0$ there is $N$ such that every
  polynomial $p$ with $\deg p=n\ge N$ and connected $\{|p|\le1\}$ satisfies
  $|p'(z)|\le(\tfrac12+\varepsilon)n^2$ on it." This follows the page's words exactly.
- **[defect: missing-normalization]** Without a normalization the statement is false.
  1. **Chebyshev.** $T_n$ has critical points $\cos(k\pi/n)$ with critical values $\pm1$. A
     lemniscate is connected iff it contains all critical points, so $\{|T_n|\le1\}$ is
     connected. It contains $1$, and $T_n'(1)=n^2>(\tfrac12+\varepsilon)n^2$ for
     $\varepsilon<\tfrac12$.
  2. **Scaling.** For $p=az^n$ the lemniscate is the disc of radius $|a|^{-1/n}$, and
     $\max|p'|=n|a|^{1/n}$ is unbounded in $a$.
- **The page means monic $p$.**
  - "The lower bound is easy: this is $\ge n$" fails for $p=az^n$ with $|a|<1$.
  - "Chebyshev polynomials show that $n^2/2$ is best possible" makes sense only after
    rescaling to monic. Take $q(z)=T_n(z/\lambda)$ with $\lambda=2^{(n-1)/n}$. Then $q$ is
    monic, and $|q'(\lambda)|=n^2/\lambda=2^{1/n}\,n^2/2$.
  - Eremenko–Lempert's theorem is the monic, capacity-1 statement.
- **v2.** v2 inserts `p.Monic →` and changes nothing else. A leading coefficient of modulus 1
  would be equivalent: multiplying by a unimodular constant changes neither $|p|$ nor $|p'|$.
- **Numerical check** (pure Python, $n=1,\dots,6$):
  - $q$ is monic, $T_n'(1)=n^2$, and the critical values have modulus 1;
  - $|q'(\lambda)|=2^{1/n}n^2/2$;
  - a grid maximum of $|q'|$ over $\{|q|\le1\}$ agrees to within grid resolution;
  - for the page's $z^2+10z+1$: critical value $-24$, and $|p'|=2\sqrt{24}\approx9.80$ at
    both roots.

  generalizes the page's example $z^2+10z+1$.
