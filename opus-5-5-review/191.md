## Summary

The page (PROVED) asks whether, for every $C>0$ and large $n$, every 2-colouring of the pairs of $\{2,\dots,n\}$ has a
monochromatic $X$ with $\sum_{x\in X}1/\log x\ge C$. Rödl proved yes [Ro03], with a matching construction of order
$\log\log\log n$ and a negative answer for 3 colours; Conlon, Fox and Sudakov proved $2^{-8}\log\log\log n$ [CFS13].

The Lean asserts: for every $C>0$ there is $N$ such that for $n\ge N$ and every graph `G` on `ℕ`, some $X\subseteq[2,n]$ that
is a clique of `G` or of `Gᶜ` has $\sum_{x\in X}1/\log x\ge C$.
