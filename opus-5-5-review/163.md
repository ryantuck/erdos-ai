## Summary

The page (PROVED) states the Burr–Erdős conjecture: if every subgraph of $H$ has a vertex of degree at most $d$, then
$R(H)\ll_d n$. It records two equivalent forms (unions of $c$ forests; average degree at most $d$), Lee's proof with
$R(H)\le2^{2^{O(d)}}n$ and the sharper $R(H)\le2^{d2^{O(\chi(H))}}n$, and the conjecture $R(H)\le2^{O(d)}n$.

The Lean states the conjecture for graphs on `Fin n` with the diagonal Ramsey number defined by graphs and complements.
