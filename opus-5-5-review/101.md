## Summary

The page conjectures (OPEN, \$100) that $n$ points in the plane with no five on a line
determine $o(n^2)$ lines through four of them. The Lean asserts that for all $\varepsilon>0$
there is $N$ such that every such $P$ with $|P|=n\ge N$ has at most $\varepsilon n^2$ lines
$L$ with $\dim L=1$ and $|P\cap L|\ge4$. That is the same proposition. v2 keeps it
byte-identical and adds context and variants.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Exactly four versus at least four.** With no five collinear, "lines containing four
  points" means exactly four, which equals at least four. The input counts $\ge4$, which is
  correct.
- **Uniform $\varepsilon$–$N$ form.** The bound holds for every admissible $P$ of size
  $n\ge N$, which is the statement that the *maximum* over $P$ is $o(n^2)$. That is the
  intended worst-case reading.
- **Not vacuous.** Admissible sets exist for all $n$, e.g. points in general position, and
  [SoSt13] shows the count can be nearly quadratic. The statement has real content.

**A2. Lean semantics traps: pass.**

- **The outer `Set.ncard` is over lines.** Each counted line contains two distinct points
  of $P$, so it equals their affine span. Hence the set has at most $\binom n2$ elements,
  is finite, and `ncard` is its true size.
- **The inner `Set.ncard`** is of $P\cap L$, which is finite.
- `Module.finrank ℝ L.direction = 1` excludes the empty subspace, points (`finrank 0`) and
  the plane (`finrank 2`). In a 2-dimensional space, `finrank` is a faithful dimension
  count.
