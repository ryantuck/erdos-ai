## Summary

The page (DISPROVED) asks whether the complement of every 3-AP-free $A\subset\mathbb R$ contains an infinite arithmetic
progression; Baumgartner showed no, using a basis of $\mathbb R$ over $\mathbb Q$.

The Lean defines 3-term progressions ($a,a+d,a+2d$, $d\ne0$) and infinite ones ($a+nd$, $n\in\mathbb N$, $d\ne0$), and asserts
that some $A$ has neither a 3-term progression nor an infinite progression in its complement.
