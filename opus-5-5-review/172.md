## Summary

The page (OPEN) asks whether every finite colouring of $\mathbb N$ admits arbitrarily large finite $A$ whose sums and products of
distinct elements are monochromatic. It records Hindman's 7-colour counterexample for infinite $A$, Erdős's two-colour infinite
question, Moreira's $\{x,x+y,xy\}$, and Alweiss's positive answer over $\mathbb Q\setminus\{0\}$ (after Bowen and Sabok's case
$|A|=2$).

The Lean asserts the "yes" direction for colourings `f : ℕ → Fin k`, constraining the sums and products of subsets with at least
two elements.
