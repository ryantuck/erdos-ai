## A. Semantic fidelity

  point $p_i$, $p_i(j)=i$ for $j\in S$ and $p_i(j)=c(j)$ otherwise, lies in $A$."

  - *Edge cases.* $t=1$: proved true (`t_one`). $\varepsilon>1$: no $A$ qualifies (vacuous, harmless).
