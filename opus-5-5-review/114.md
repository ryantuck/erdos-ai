## Summary

The page asks (OPEN, \$250) whether, among monic $p\in\mathbb C[z]$ of degree $n$, the length
of $\{|p(z)|=1\}$ is maximised by $z^n-1$. It records the progress:

- $f(n)\le4\pi n$ [Do61], $\ll n^2$ [Po61], $\ll n$ [Bo95];
- $9.173n$ and the case $n=2$ [ErHa99];

- Tao [Ta25]: the conjecture holds, with uniqueness, for all large $n$.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Source** (verbatim): "If $p(z)\in\mathbb{C}[z]$ is a monic polynomial of degree $n$ then
  is the length of the curve $\{ z\in \mathbb{C} : \lvert p(z)\rvert=1\}$ maximised when
  $p(z)=z^n-1$?"
- **Back-translation:** "for every $n\ge1$ and monic $p$ of degree $n$,
  $\mathcal H^1(\{|p|=1\})\le\mathcal H^1(\{|z^n-1|=1\})$." That is exactly "maximised at
  $z^n-1$".
- **Length is right.** A lemniscate is a finite union of analytic arcs, so its
  1-dimensional Hausdorff measure is its length.

**A2. Lean semantics traps: pass.**

- **Normalisation.** Mathlib's `μH[d]` is unnormalised (`mkMetric (r ^ d)`). For $d=1$ it is
  the true length:
  - `hausdorffMeasure_real : (μH[1] : Measure ℝ) = volume`;
  - isometry invariance carries this to segments in $\mathbb C$.
- **No ∞ ≤ ∞ vacuity.**
  - Both sides are finite: every such lemniscate has length at most $2\pi n$ [Da07].
  - The right side is $2n+O(1)$, and the left side being $\infty$ would falsify, not
    trivialise, the inequality.
- **The guard `1 ≤ n` is necessary.**
  - For $n=0$ the only monic $p$ is $1$. Its level set is $\mathbb C$, of measure $\infty$.
  - Meanwhile $X^0-1=0$ has empty level set.
  - Without the guard the theorem would be false.
- **The comparison polynomial.** `(X : ℂ[X]) ^ n - 1` is monic of degree $n$ for $n\ge1$, so
  the bound is attained and is not vacuous.
- **`‖p.eval z‖`** is the complex modulus.

**A3. Definitions: pass.** `levelCurveUnit` and `arcLength` match their docstrings. No
`sorry` in definitions.

**A4. Question form and answer polarity: pass.** The question is a yes/no question with

- **Uniqueness in `large_n`.** The monic polynomials whose lemniscate is a rotated and
  translated copy of $\{|z^n-1|=1\}$ are exactly $(z-c)^n-\omega$ with $|\omega|=1$:
  - the motion $w=e^{i\theta}z+c$ maps the level set of $z^n-1$ to that of the monic
    $(w-c)^n-e^{in\theta}$;
  - a monic polynomial is determined by its lemniscate.
- **Sanity checks for `pommerenke_connected`:**
  - $p=z^n$ attains $2\pi$;
  - for $p=z^2-1$ the open set is two lobes, so the hypothesis fails, as it should;
  - the standard argument goes through the convex hull: perimeter $\ge2\pi\cdot$capacity,
    and the capacity is $1$ for monic $p$.
- **Not formalized:**
  - the bounds $4\pi n$, $\ll n^2$, $\ll n$ and $9.173n$, all superseded by `danchenko`;
  - local maximality [FrNa09];
  - the asymptotic $\mathcal H^1(\{|z^n-1|=1\})=2n+O(1)$.
