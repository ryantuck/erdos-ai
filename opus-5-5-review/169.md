## Summary

The page (OPEN) defines $f(k)$ as the supremum of $\sum_{n\in A}1/n$ over sets of positive integers without a $k$-term
progression, asks to estimate it, and asks whether $f(k)/\log W(k)\to\infty$. It records Berlekamp's and Gerver's lower bounds, the
trivial bound $\frac12$, Gerver's equivalence of Problem [3] with the finiteness of $f$, records for $f(3)$ and $f(4)$, and Walker's
reduction to Kempner sets.

The Lean defines AP-freeness for finsets, the van der Waerden property for $2$-colourings of $\{0,\dots,N-1\}$, $W(k)$ by
`Nat.find` from a `sorry`-proved existence lemma, and states the unrolled limit.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Source** (verbatim): "Let $k\geq 3$ and $f(k)$ be the supremum of $\sum_{n\in A}\frac{1}{n}$ as $A$ ranges over all sets of
  positive integers which do not contain a $k$-term arithmetic progression. Estimate $f(k)$. Is\[\lim_{k\to \infty}\frac{f(k)}{\log
  W(k)}=\infty\]where $W(k)$ is the van der Waerden number?"
- **Back-translation** (lines 39–43): "For every $C>0$ there is $k_0$ such that for every $k\ge k_0$ some finite set of positive
  integers with no $k$-term progression has $\sum1/n>C\log W(k)$."
- **Checks.**
  - *Finite sets.* The reciprocal sum of a set of positive integers is the supremum of the sums over its finite subsets, and
    subsets of a $k$-AP-free set are $k$-AP-free. So $f(k)$ is the supremum over finite sets, and "$f(k)>C\log W(k)$" is "some
    finite set exceeds $C\log W(k)$". This also covers $f(k)=\infty$.
  - *The limit.* $\log W(k)>0$ for $k\ge2$ ($W(2)=3$), so "$f(k)/\log W(k)\to\infty$" is "for all $C>0$, eventually
    $f(k)>C\log W(k)$".
  - *`IsAPFree`* excludes $d=0$; with `0 < n` on `A`, every progression is one of positive integers.
  - *`VDWProperty k N`* asks every `f : ℕ → Bool` for a monochromatic progression with last term `< N`, so only the colours on
    $\{0,\dots,N-1\}$ matter; it is the usual $W(k)=W(k;2)$, shifted by one. Tests: $W(0)=1$, $W(1)=1$, $W(2)=3$ (proved).

**A2. Lean semantics traps: pass.** `k - 1` truncates only at $k=0$, where the definition gives $W(0)=1$ (harmless). `Nat.find`
uses `open Classical in`.

**A3. Definitions: DEFECT (fixed).**

- `IsAPFree`, `VDWProperty` and `vanDerWaerdenNumber` match their docstrings and the page.
- **[defect: sorry-in-definition]** `vanDerWaerdenNumber` is `Nat.find (vdw_exists k)`, and `vdw_exists` is `by sorry` (input line
  20).
  - The value of `Nat.find` does not depend on which proof is supplied, so the meaning is right. But the definition and anything
    proved about it depend on `sorryAx`, and nothing in the build flags this beyond a warning on the lemma.
  - **Fix, proved in v2.** Mathlib's `Combinatorics.Line.exists_mono_in_high_dimension (Fin k) Bool` gives a finite `ι` such that
    every $2$-colouring of `ι → Fin k` has a monochromatic combinatorial line. Colour `v` by `f (∑ i, v i)`. A line `l` with moving
    coordinates `s` maps `x : Fin k` to $b+|s|\,x$, where $b$ is the sum of the fixed coordinates. So the line gives a
    monochromatic progression with difference $|s|>0$ and last term $\le|\iota|(k-1)$, and $N=|\iota|(k-1)+1$ works. $k=0$ is
    handled separately.
