## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Source** (verbatim): "In any $2$-colouring of $\mathbb{R}^2$, for all but at most one triangle $T$, there is a monochromatic
  congruent copy of $T$."
- **Back-translation** (lines 39–48): "For every $f:\mathbb R^2\to\{0,1\}$ and non-degenerate triangles $p,q$: if no triple with
  the squared side lengths of $p$ is monochromatic, and likewise for $q$, then $p$ and $q$ have the same squared side lengths."
- **Checks.**
  - *Congruence.* Two triples of points in the plane with the same multiset of pairwise distances are congruent (SSS; it holds
    even for collinear triples). Monochromaticity of a copy does not depend on which congruent triangle is used, so "all but at
    most one triangle" can only mean up to congruence.
  - *Copies are triangles.* A triple with the side lengths of a non-degenerate triangle is non-degenerate
    (`nonDegenerate_iff_of_sideSqs_eq`, from Heron's formula `four_mul_cross_sq`). So "copy" needs no separate condition.
  - *Labelling.* `triangleSideSqs` lists the three pairwise distances, so the multiset does not depend on the order of the
    vertices (`sideSqs_swap₁₂`).
  - *"At most one".* Two exceptional triangles must be congruent. Degenerate triples are excluded, as "triangle" requires.
  - *Colourings.* `ℝ × ℝ → Bool`, with no regularity assumption, as in the problem.
