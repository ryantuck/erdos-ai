## Summary

The page (OPEN) asks for the smallest $k$ such that $\mathbb R^2$ has a red/blue colouring with no two red points at distance $1$
and no blue $k$-term progression with step length $1$. Known: $k\ge6$ [Ts17]; Erdős and Graham claim $k\le10^7$ without proof;
Alon showed that without the step-length restriction no $k$ exists.

The Lean asserts: there are $k$ and $f:\mathbb R^2\to\mathrm{Bool}$ with no two `true` points at distance $1$ and no
$p,v$ with $\lVert v\rVert=1$ and $f(p+iv)=$ `false` for all $i<k$.
