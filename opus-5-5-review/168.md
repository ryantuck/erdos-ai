## Summary

The page (OPEN) asks for $\lim F(N)/N$, where $F(N)$ is the largest size of a subset of $\{1,\dots,N\}$ with no $\{n,2n,3n\}$, and
whether this limit is irrational. It records the [GSW77] formula $\frac13\sum_{k\in K}1/d_k$ over the $3$-smooth numbers $d_k$, and
Eberhard's value $0.800965\ldots$.

The Lean defines $F$ as a `Finset.sup` over the powerset of `Icc 1 N`, and asserts that $F(N)/N$ tends to an irrational $\alpha$.
