## Summary

The page (OPEN) asks for the best $f(d)$ such that every 2-colouring of $\mathbb Z$ has, for infinitely many $d$, a monochromatic
progression with common difference $d$ and length $f(d)$. Erdős's $\sqrt2$ colouring gives $f(d)\ll d$, Beck's random colouring
$f(d)\le(1+o(1))\log_2d$, and van der Waerden's theorem $f(d)\to\infty$.

The Lean defines `hasMonoAP χ d k` (a monochromatic $k$-term progression with difference $d$) and asserts: there is $c>0$ such
that every colouring has, for infinitely many $d$, such a progression of length at least $c\log d$.

## A. Semantic fidelity

- **Non-degeneracy.** `hasMonoAP χ 0 k` holds for every $k$ (`hasMonoAP_zero`, proved), so the requirement $d>N$ for every $N$ is
