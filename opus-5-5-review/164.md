## Summary

The page (PROVED) asks whether $\sum_{n\in A}\frac1{n\log n}$ is maximised over primitive sets $A$ (no element divides
another) by the primes. It records that Erdős [Er35] proved the sum converges for every primitive set and that Lichtman [Li23]
proved the answer is yes.

The Lean defines primitivity and states the inequality against the prime sum for every primitive $A\subseteq\{2,3,\dots\}$,
with `tsum` over subtypes.
