## Summary

The page states (PROVED) that every $A\subseteq\mathbb N$ of positive upper density
contains $B+C$ with $B$ and $C$ infinite. The Lean defines the upper density as the
`limsup` of $|A\cap[0,n)|/n$, defines the sumset, and asserts exactly that. v2 is the input
plus scholarship.
