## Summary

The page (PROVED) asks whether, for any infinite family of infinite sets $A_i=\{a_{i1}<a_{i2}<\cdots\}$, some
$f:\mathbb N\to\{-1,1\}$ has all partial sums along $A_1,\dots,A_d$ bounded by a constant depending on $d$. Beck [Be81] proved it;
Beck [Be17] improved the bound to $\ll d^{4+\epsilon}$.

The Lean quantifies over families `a : ℕ → ℕ → ℕ` of strictly increasing sequences and asserts the existence of such an $f$.

  $A_i=\{a_{i1}<a_{i2}<\cdots\}$. Does there exist some $f:\mathbb{N}\to\{-1,1\}$ such that\[\max_{m, 1\leq i\leq d}

- **Checks.**

    page's wording must lie in $\mathbb N$ for $f$ to be defined on them.

    content is in balancing all $d$ sequences with one $f$.
