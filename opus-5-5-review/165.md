## Summary

The page (OPEN, \$250) asks: "Give an asymptotic formula for $R(3,k)$." It records that
$(c+o(1))\frac{k^2}{\log k}\le R(3,k)\le(1+o(1))\frac{k^2}{\log k}$, with the lower bound due to Kim and the upper bound to Shearer,
and the history of the constant: $1/162$ (Kim), $1/4$ (Bohman–Keevash; Fiz Pontiveros–Griffiths–Morris), $1/3$ (Campos, Jenssen,
Michelen and Sahasrabudhe), $1/2$ (Hefty, Horn, King and Pfender). The last two papers conjecture $c=1/2$.

The Lean defines `ramseyR3 k` as an `sInf` and states the existence of $c>0$ with $(c\pm\varepsilon)\,k^2/\log k$ bounds for large
$k$.
