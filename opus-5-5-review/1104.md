## Summary

The source (OPEN) reads: "Let $f(n)$ be the maximum possible chromatic number of a
triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices. Estimate $f(n)$." The page gives the best bounds
$(1-o(1))(n/\log n)^{1/2}\le f(n)\le(2+o(1))(n/\log n)^{1/2}$: the upper bound is from
Davies–Illingworth and the lower from Hefty–Horn–King–Pfender's construction.
