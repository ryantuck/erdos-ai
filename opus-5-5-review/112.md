## Summary

The page asks (OPEN) to determine the least $k(n,m)$ such that every directed graph on $k$
vertices has an independent set of size $n$ or a transitive tournament of size $m$. It lists
the known bounds:

- Erdős–Rado $\frac{2^{m-1}(n-1)^m+n-2}{2n-3}$;
- Larson–Mitchell $k(n,3)\le n^2$;
- Hunter's $R(n,m)\le k(n,m)\le R(n,m,m)\le3^{n+2m}$.

The input defines $k$ via `sInf` and states the Erdős–Rado bound. Because of the 2-cycle
issue, its $k$ is identically $0$. v2 fixes the definition and states the bound as the proved
result it is. The "determine" question has no conjectured formula, so it has no formal
target, as with 1104 in the previous Opus batch.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: DEFECT (fixed).**

- **[defect: vacuous-definition]** Take $V$ with $|V|=k\ge2$ and `adj u v := u ≠ v`. This
  satisfies `loopless`.
  - `IsIndepSet S` fails for every $|S|\ge2$.
  - `IsTransTournament S` fails for every $|S|\ge2$: for $i<j$ it needs
    `adj (f j) (f i) ↔ j < i`, i.e. `True ↔ False`.
  - For $k\le1$ there is no $n$-set at all, since $n\ge2$.
  - Hence `{k | …} = ∅` for every $n,m\ge2$, and `dirRamseyNum n m = 0` (`Nat.sInf_empty`).
  - Every upper-bound statement about it holds trivially, and the input's theorem is
    content-free.
- **The fix: oriented graphs.**
  - Erdős–Rado's directed graphs are oriented. Hunter's $k\le R(n,m,m)$ argument colours
    each pair by none, forward or backward, which presupposes at most one arc.
  - v2 adds `antisymm : ∀ u v, adj u v → ¬ adj v u`.
  - Under orientation, the `↔` in `IsTransTournament` is equivalent to the natural
    `i < j → adj (f i) (f j)`, so S induces a transitive tournament.
  - Allowing 2-cycles but reading "contains" non-inducedly would give the same $k$, since
    deleting one arc of each 2-cycle changes nothing relevant. The docstring records this.
- **Brute force under v2's definitions** (scratch script, all oriented graphs):
  - $k(2,2)=2$;
  - $k(2,3)=4$: every 4-tournament has a transitive triple, and the cyclic triangle has
    none;
  - $k(3,2)=3$.

  The complete symmetric digraph on 4 vertices has neither structure under the input's
  definitions, as claimed.

**A2. Lean semantics traps: pass, apart from the `sInf ∅` trap above.**

- **The Erdős–Rado bound in ℕ.**
  - Subtractions are exact for $n,m\ge2$: $m-1,\ n-1\ge1$, the numerator sum is $\ge2$,
    and $2n-3\ge1$.
  - Floor division is as strong as the real bound, because $k$ is an integer.
  - Spot check: at $(2,3)$ the bound is $4=k(2,3)$, which is tight.

**A3. Definitions: DEFECT (fixed).** `Digraph` lacked antisymmetry; see A1. The other
definitions match their docstrings once orientation holds. No `sorry` in definitions.

**A4. Question form and answer polarity: no formal target for the open part.** "Determine
