## Summary

The page (OPEN, no prize) defines $\tau(G)$ as the least number of vertices meeting every maximal clique of $G$ on
at least two vertices, $H(n)$ as the largest number such that every triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices has an
independent set of that size, and asks whether $\tau(G)\le n-H(n)$ for every graph on $n$ vertices. It records
that $\tau(G)\le n-\sqrt n$ is easy and that the triangle-free case is trivial, that Erdős and Gallai could not make
progress even for $K_4$-free graphs and that Erdős called the conjecture "perhaps completely wrongheaded", that it
reappears as Problem 1 of [EGT92], and that the general behaviour of $\tau(G)$ is the subject of [610].

The Lean defines maximal cliques, clique transversals, $\tau$, independent sets, $\alpha$ and $H$ on `Fin n`, and states
the inequality for all `n` and all graphs.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Source** (verbatim): "For a graph $G$ let $\tau(G)$ denote the minimal number of vertices that include at least
  one from each maximal clique of $G$ on at least two vertices (sometimes called the clique transversal number).
  Let $H(n)$ be maximal such that every triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices contains an independent set on $H(n)$
  vertices. If $G$ is a graph on $n$ vertices then is $\tau(G)\leq n-H(n)$?"
- **Back-translation of the input's theorem:** "For every $n$ and every graph $G$ on $\{0,\dots,n-1\}$, $\tau(G)\le
  n-H(n)$, where $\tau(G)$ is the least size of a vertex set meeting every inclusion-maximal clique with at least two
  vertices, and $H(n)$ is the least independence number of a triangle-free graph on $n$ vertices."
- **Checks.**
  - *Maximal clique.* `IsMaximalCliqueFS G S` is "`S` is a clique and no vertex outside `S` extends it", which is
    inclusion-maximality (not maximum size). The guard `2 ≤ S.card` in `IsCliqueTransversal` drops the maximal cliques
    with one vertex, the isolated vertices, as on the page.
  - *$\tau$.* `cliqueTransversalNumber G` is the `sInf` of the sizes of clique transversals. `Finset.univ` is one (every
    maximal clique with at least two vertices is nonempty), so the set is nonempty and `sInf` is its least element.
  - *$H(n)$.* The page's "maximal such that every triangle-free graph contains an independent set on $H(n)$ vertices"
    is the minimum, over triangle-free graphs on $n$ vertices, of the independence number, which is the input's
    `sInf`. The edgeless graph is triangle-free, so the set is nonempty. Exact values: $H(n)=1,1,2,2,2,3,3$ for
    $n=1,\dots,7$ (Addendum), which are $\max\{k:R(3,k)\le n\}$ for the Ramsey numbers $R(3,3)=6$, $R(3,4)=9$.
  - *$\alpha$.* `independenceNumber` is the `sSup` of the sizes of independent sets, a nonempty bounded set of
    naturals. `erdos_problem_151.exists_max_indep` proves in Lean that a maximum independent set exists.
  - *Subtraction.* `n - H n` is subtraction of natural numbers. `variants.H_le` proves in Lean that `H n ≤ n`, so
    nothing is truncated.
  - *Direction.* The page asks "is $\tau(G)\le n-H(n)$?" and the input asserts yes, the asked direction. Erdős doubted
    it, but the problem is open, so the conjecture is the right statement.
  - *Not vacuous and not false at small parameters.* An exact search over every labelled graph on at most 8 vertices
    finds $\tau(G)\le n-H(n)$ for all of them, and the maximum of $\tau$ over graphs on $n$ vertices equals $n-H(n)$
    for every $n\le8$ ($0,1,1,2,3,3,4,5$). So the inequality is sharp in the range, and the statement is not trivially
    true. It is also not trivially false: the triangle-free case follows from the definitions
    (`variants.triangle_free_case`).
  - *The page's remark with $\sqrt n$.* "It is easy to see that $\tau(G)\le n-\sqrt n$" fails for $n=2$
    ($\tau(K_2)=1>2-\sqrt2$; `variants.tau_le_sqrt_false` proves it in Lean) and for $n=5$ ($\tau(C_5)=3>5-\sqrt5=2.76$;
    the 12 labelled copies of $C_5$ are the only violations on 5 vertices). It is an asymptotic remark; the page of
    [610] records the bound $\tau(G)\le n-\sqrt{2n}+O(1)$ of [EGT92], and v2 states the remark with an additive
    constant (`variants.easy_bound`). **DEFERRED:** the exact form in [EGT92].
