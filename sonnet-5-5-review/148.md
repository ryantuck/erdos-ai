## Summary

The page (OPEN, no prize) defines $F(k)$ as the number of solutions of $1=\frac1{n_1}+\cdots+\frac1{n_k}$ in
distinct integers $1\le n_1<\cdots<n_k$ and asks for good estimates. It records
$2^{c^{k/\log k}}\le F(k)\le c_0^{(1/5+o(1))2^k}$, the lower bound by Konyagin and the upper bound by Elsholtz and
Planitzer, with $c_0=1.26408\cdots$ the 'Vardi constant'.

The Lean defines $F(k)$ correctly, states the two bounds, and states a guess of its own as "the problem".

    rest is a solution with $k-1$ terms for the target $r-1/m$.
