## Summary

The page (OPEN at capture) conjectures that for a bipartite $r$-degenerate $H$, $\mathrm{ex}(n;H)\ll n^{2-1/r}$.
It records that this is open even for $r=2$, that Alon, Krivelevich and Sudakov [AKS03] proved
$\mathrm{ex}(n;H)\ll n^{2-1/4r}$, and that they also prove the full bound when the maximum degree on one side of
the bipartition is $r$.

The input asserts the conjecture. It has since been refuted already for $r=2$, so v2 asserts its negation.

## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass for the proposition; see A4/A5.**

- **Source** (verbatim): "If $H$ is bipartite and is $r$-degenerate, that is, every induced subgraph of $H$ has
  minimum degree $\leq r$, then $\mathrm{ex}(n;H) \ll n^{2-1/r}.$"
- **Back-translation of the input's theorem:** "For every $r\ge1$ and every finite simple graph $H$ that is
  $2$-colourable and $r$-degenerate there is $C>0$ such that for all $n\ge1$ the maximum number of edges of an
  $n$-vertex graph with no copy of $H$ is at most $C\,n^{2-1/r}$."
- **Checks.**
  - *Bipartite.* `Nonempty (H.Coloring (Fin 2))` is a proper $2$-colouring, which is bipartiteness.
  - *$r$-degenerate.* `IsRDegenerateGraph H r` says that every non-empty finite vertex set has a vertex with at
    most $r$ neighbours inside it. For a finite graph this is "every induced subgraph has minimum degree
    $\le r$", the page's definition. It is monotone in $r$ (a $2$-degenerate graph is $3$-degenerate), as the
    page's reading requires.
  - *The Turán number.* `turanNumber146 H n` is the maximum edge count of a graph on $n$ vertices with no
    injective homomorphic copy of $H$. `variants.containsSubgraph_iff` proves in Lean that "contains a copy"
    is Mathlib's `H ⊑ F`, and `variants.turanNumber_eq_extremalNumber` proves that `turanNumber146 H n` equals
    Mathlib's `SimpleGraph.extremalNumber n H`. `variants.mantel_four` proves `ex(4; K₃) = 4`, Mantel's value.
  - *"$\ll$".* For fixed $r$ and $H$, $f\ll g$ means $f\le Cg$ for large $n$, and the finitely many small $n$ are
    absorbed in $C$, so `∃ C > 0, ∀ n ≥ 1, … ≤ C * n ^ (2 - 1 / r)` is the same statement. The constant may
    depend on $r$ and $H$, as the page intends, since `∃ C` comes after the quantifiers over `r` and `H`.
  - *Degenerate cases.* For $r=1$ the exponent is $1$, and $1$-degenerate bipartite graphs are forests, for which
    $\mathrm{ex}(n;H)=O(n)$. If $H$ is edgeless with at most $n$ vertices, every $n$-vertex graph contains $H$,
    the family of $H$-free graphs is empty, and both `turanNumber146` and `extremalNumber` return `0`. That
    cannot affect the bound.
  - *Universe.* The theorem quantifies over `U : Type*`, and the graph families inside `turanNumber146` use
    `V : Type`. A graph on `n` vertices has such a representative, so nothing is lost.

**A2. Lean semantics traps: pass.**

  non-empty. It is empty only in the degenerate case above, where the value is `0`. The bridge to
