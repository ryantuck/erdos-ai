## Summary

The page (OPEN) asks two questions about the gaps between consecutive squarefree numbers $s_1<s_2<\cdots$: is
$s_{n+1}-s_n\ll_\epsilon s_n^\epsilon$ for every $\epsilon>0$, and is
$s_{n+1}-s_n\le(1+o(1))\frac{\pi^2}{6}\frac{\log s_n}{\log\log s_n}$?

The Lean has two theorems, `erdos_problem_208a` ($\exists C>0,\exists N_0,\forall n\ge N_0$: gap $\le Cs_n^\varepsilon$) and
`erdos_problem_208b` ($\forall\delta>0,\exists N_0,\forall n\ge N_0$: gap $\le(1+\delta)\frac{\pi^2}{6}\log s_n/\log\log s_n$),
about a sequence `squarefreeSeq` that is `sorry` plus three axioms.
