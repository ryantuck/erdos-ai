## A. Semantic fidelity

**A1. Statement fidelity: pass.**

- **Source** (verbatim): "Is there an integer $m\geq 1$ with $(m,6)=1$ such that none of $2^k3^\ell m+1$ are prime, for any
  $k,\ell\geq 0$?"
- **Back-translation** (lines 13–16): "There is a natural number $m\ge1$, coprime to 6, such that $2^k3^\ell m+1$ is not prime
  for any natural numbers $k,\ell$."
- **Checks.**
  - *Redundant hypothesis.* `Nat.Coprime 0 6` is false ($\gcd(0,6)=6$), so `1 ≤ m` follows from coprimality
    (`pos_of_coprime`, proved); upstream omits it.
  - *The $\ell=0$ slice.* For $\ell=0$ the condition says that $m$ is a Sierpiński number. `sierpinski_78557` (proved) shows
    $2^k\cdot78557+1$ is never prime: writing $k=36q+r$, $2^{36}\equiv1$ modulo each of $3,5,7,13,19,37,73$, and for each
    $r<36$ one of these primes divides $2^r\cdot78557+1$ (3 for even $r$; 5 for $r\equiv1\pmod4$; 7 for $r\equiv7\pmod{12}$;
    13 for $r\equiv11\pmod{12}$; 19, 37, 73 for $r=15,27,3$). So `variants.sierpinski` (proved) answers the $\ell=0$
    restriction.
  - *Not vacuous or settled by a known number.* `not_78557` (proved): $k=13$, $\ell=1$ gives the prime $1930616833$. `small_m`
    (proved): for every $m<100$ coprime to 6 some $2^k3^\ell m+1$ with $k\le3$, $\ell\le1$ is prime. A computation
    (scratchpad) extends this to $m<1000$, the largest least prime being $118747$ for $m=733$.
  - *Equivalent form.* The statement says that $3^\ell m$ is a Sierpiński number for every $\ell\ge0$ (for $k=0$ the number
    $3^\ell m+1$ is even, and $>2$ unless $m=1$, $\ell=0$).
