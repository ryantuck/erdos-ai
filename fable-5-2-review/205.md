## Summary

The page (DISPROVED, Lean) asks whether every large $n$ is $2^k+m$ with $\Omega(m)<\log\log m$, and whether this holds with
$\epsilon\log\log m$ or a slower function. Barreto and Leeham proved the answer is no; Tao and Alexeev showed that infinitely
many $n$ have $\Omega(n-2^k)\gg(\log n/\log\log n)^{1/2}$ for all $2^k<n$.

The Lean asserts: for every $N$ there is $n\ge N$ such that for all $k$ with $2^k\le n$,
$\log\log(n-2^k)\le\Omega(n-2^k)$, with $\Omega$ the length of `primeFactorsList`.
