## Summary

The problem asks: "How fast must an infinite sequence $A$ grow if every element of its sumset $A + A$ is squarefree?" Erdős notes that such sequences exist with exponential growth but expresses doubt about polynomial-growth sequences. The source page (OPEN status) cites known bounds: van Doorn–Tao proved $a_j > 0.24 j^{4/3}$ and constructed sequences with $a_j < \exp(5j/\log j)$; Konyagin proved $a_j \gg j^{15/11-o(1)}$ for the finite analogue. The Lean formalization encodes Erdős's expectation as a direct assertion: any such sequence must grow faster than any polynomial, i.e., $\forall C > 0, \exists N, \forall j \ge N: a(j) > j^C$. The statement is mathematically correct; the sole defect is a missing citation.
