## Summary

The problem asks: for r ≥ 3, what is the minimal d such that the r-uniform Turán extremal number ex_r(n; d, e) = o(n²)? The conjecture is d_r(e) = (r-2)·e + 3. The Lean formalization defines r-uniform hypergraphs as sets of r-element subsets, configuration avoidance via a "contains configuration" predicate, the Turán number as the supremum of edge counts over non-avoiding hypergraphs, and the minimal d as the infimum over d-values where the Turán number is asymptotically o(n²). The theorem states the conjectured equality. The definitions are correct and the statement is a faithful translation of the English problem. The sole defect is that citation keys [BES73], [Er75b], [Er81] are used in docstrings but never defined in the module docstring's bibliography section — this must be fixed.
