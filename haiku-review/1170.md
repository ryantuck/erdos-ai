## Summary

The problem asks: "Is it consistent (with ZFC) that ω₂ → (α)²₂ for every α < ω₂?" — a consistency question about the ordinal partition relation. The Lean file formalizes the *property* itself: a set-theoretic definition of the partition relation κ → (α)²₂ via strictly monotone embeddings, and a theorem asserting it for ω₂ and all α < ω₂. The definitions are mathematically correct, the strategy (formalizing the property rather than the meta-theoretic consistency claim) is sound. However, two defects must be fixed: (1) the theorem lacks the `answer(sorry) ↔` wrapper required for yes/no questions by the checklist and corpus convention; (2) citations [La82] and [FoHa03] appear in the docstring but are never defined, and [Va99] is missing entirely.
