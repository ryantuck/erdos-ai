## Summary

The problem consists of two related yes/no questions about existence of graphs with specific Ramsey-theoretic properties:

**Part 1:** Does there exist a graph G with no K₄ such that every edge coloring with countably many colors contains a monochromatic K₃?

**Part 2:** Does there exist a graph G with no K_{ℵ₁} such that every edge coloring with countably many colors contains a monochromatic K_{ℵ₀}?

Both are open problems of Erdős and Hajnal; Shelah proved existence can be consistent with ZFC. The Lean formalization correctly captures the graph-theoretic content (cliques, colorings, cardinalities) but encodes both as bare existential assertions rather than the required `answer(sorry) ↔ P` form for yes/no questions. This is the primary defect.
