## Summary

The problem asks: "Do there exist infinitely many natural numbers n such that n − 2x² is prime
for all x with 2x² < n?" The problem was disproved by Epure and Gica (2010); only finitely many
such n exist: {2, 5, 7, 13, 31, 61, 181, 199} (with at most one exception). The Lean file
correctly formalizes this as `Set.Finite {n : ℕ | AllShiftsArePrime n}`, where the helper
predicate `AllShiftsArePrime n` encodes the primality condition. However, the theorem statement
is missing the `answer(False) ↔` wrapper required by the corpus convention for yes/no questions.
The mathematical content is correct; the encoding is defective.
