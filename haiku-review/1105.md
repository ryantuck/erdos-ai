## Summary

Problem 1105 concerns the anti-Ramsey number AR(n,G), the maximum number of colors in an edge-coloring of K_n that avoids a rainbow copy of G (one where all edges have distinct colors). The problem poses two conjectures about cycles C_k and paths P_k:
1. For cycles: AR(n, C_k) = ((k-2)/2 + 1/(k-1)) n + O(1)?
2. For paths P_k (k ≥ 5): AR(n, P_k) = max(C(k-2,2) + 1, C(ℓ-1,2) + (ℓ-1)(n-ℓ+1) + ε) where ℓ = ⌊(k-1)/2⌋, ε ∈ {1,2}?

The Lean file correctly captures both via definitions of the anti-Ramsey number (using sSup over feasible colorings), the path graph, and the cycle graph, with two theorems stating the conjectures as direct assertions of equality/existence (appropriate for solved problems). Both conjectures were proved affirmatively: the cycle formula by Montellano-Ballesteros and Neumann-Lara [MoNe05], and the path formula by Yuan [Yu21]. The statements are faithful translations of the English problem; the definitions are sound and handle boundary cases correctly; the theorems have no semantic defects.
