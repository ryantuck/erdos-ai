## Summary

The problem: given a real α > 0 that is not an integer, what is the natural density of positive integers n for which gcd(n, ⌊n^α⌋) = 1? The answer, proved by Bergelson and Richter, is 6/π² — the probability that two random integers are coprime, suggesting that n and ⌊n^α⌋ behave asymptotically as independent random integers. The Lean statement correctly formalizes this as a density limit: `Tendsto (fun x ↦ count(n ∈ [1,x], Coprime n ⌊n^α⌋) / x) atTop (nhds (6/π²))`.
