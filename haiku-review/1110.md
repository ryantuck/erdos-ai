## Summary

The problem: given coprime integers p > q ≥ 2, call a natural number n "representable" if it equals a sum of coprime p^a q^b terms where no term divides another. For the excluded case {p,q} ≠ {2,3}, the problem asks two things: (1) what can be said about the density of non-representable numbers? (2) are there infinitely many coprime non-representable numbers? The Lean formalization encodes the second question as a direct assertion (`theorem erdos_problem_1110 : ... → Set.Infinite {...}`), but per the review checklist (FABLE_REVIEW.md A4), a yes/no question must be encoded as `answer(True) ↔ P` where P is the question as asked. This is the only semantic defect found; all definitions are correct, quantifiers match the source, and the mathematical content is faithful.
