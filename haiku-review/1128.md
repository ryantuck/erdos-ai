## Summary

The Erdős–Hajnal problem asks: given three sets $A, B, C$ of cardinality $\aleph_1$, must any
2-coloring of $A \times B \times C$ contain a countably infinite monochromatic sub-product
$A_1 \times B_1 \times C_1$? The answer is no — Prikry and Mills disproved this in 1978 (unpublished).
The Lean formalization correctly encodes this as a direct counterexample assertion: there exist
sets of cardinality $\aleph_1$ and a 2-coloring such that for all countable sub-products, none
are monochromatic. Back-translated independently, the Lean statement and the English problem
statement agree precisely. No semantic defects found.
