The problem: let $f(N)$ be the cardinality of the largest subset $A \subseteq \{1, \ldots, N\}$ such that every element of the sumset $A + A$ is squarefree. **Is it true that $f(N) \le (\log N)^{O(1)}$?** This is an open yes/no question.
