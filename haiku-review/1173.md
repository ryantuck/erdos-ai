The problem, attributed to Erdős and Hajnal, asks: assuming the Generalized Continuum Hypothesis (GCH), given a set mapping $f : \omega_{\omega+1} \to [\omega_{\omega+1}]^{\le \aleph_\omega}$ with |f(α) ∩ f(β)| < ℵ_ω for α ≠ β, does there exist a free set of cardinality ℵ_{ω+1}? The formalization correctly defines GCH, the ordinal ω_{ω+1}, the notion of a free set, and the cardinality constraints with proper universe lifting. However, the theorem statement is a bare existential assertion (direct form) when the source question — "Does there exist…?" — requires the `answer(sorry) ↔ …` form per this corpus's uniform convention and the FABLE_REVIEW checklist (A4).
